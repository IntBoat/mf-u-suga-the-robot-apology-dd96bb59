Two lines for make.fun. Line 1 is a robot apologizing in a single sentence for one specific mistake it made. Line 2 is one sentence saying what the robot learned and what it will do differently. The entry program writes the two lines, at most 400 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59/requirements

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py`; it writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

I am sorry: I watered your grandmother's orchid with cold coffee because my plant chart listed it under office drinks.
I have learned that a label is not a watering can, so from now on I will ask before I pour anything on anything alive.
